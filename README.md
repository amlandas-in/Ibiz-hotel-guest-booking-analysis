# 🏨 Ibiz Hotel – Guest Booking Analysis Dashboard
## 📌 Project Overview

Ibiz is a business hotel that aims to better understand guest behavior and booking trends to improve service quality and revenue performance.
This project analyzes booking data as of July 2024, focusing on the most recent three-month period (April, May, and June).

The analysis identifies frequent guests, recently active guests, and high-value customers by examining booking frequency, recency, and spending patterns. Insights from this project support data-driven decisions around guest retention, pricing strategies, and personalized service offerings.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify frequent guests based on booking history

- Segment guests by spending behavior to detect high-value customers

- Analyze recent booking activity to understand guest engagement

- Evaluate the business impact of VIP customers

- Uncover trends that help optimize services and enhance the guest experience

---

## 📊 Dashboards & Analysis
### 1️⃣ Frequent Guest Overview

Guests are segmented based on booking frequency to understand loyalty behavior and revenue contribution.

#### Guest Segments:

- Frequent Guests: 6 or more bookings

- Occasional Guests: Fewer than 6 bookings

#### Metrics Analyzed:

- Percentage share of total booking revenue

- Average booking amount

#### Key Insight:
A small group of frequent guests shows significantly higher average booking values, while occasional guests contribute more revenue through booking volume. This indicates strong potential to grow loyalty-driven revenue.
***

### 2️⃣ Spending Overview

Guests are categorized based on total spending to identify high-value customers.

#### Guest Segments:

- Top Payers: Total spend ≥ ₹50,000

- Budget Guests: Total spend < ₹50,000

#### Metrics Analyzed:

- Revenue contribution by segment

- Average booking value

#### Key Insight:
Top payers form a smaller customer base but contribute the majority of total revenue, highlighting clear opportunities for premium pricing and targeted upselling.
****

### 3️⃣ Recency Analysis

Guests are classified based on recent booking activity to measure engagement.

#### Guest Segments:

- Active Guests: Booked within the last 15 days

- Inactive Guests: No bookings in the last 15 days

#### Metrics Analyzed:

- Revenue share

- Average booking amount

#### Key Insight:
Recently active guests spend significantly more per booking, showing a strong relationship between recency and revenue potential.
****

### 4️⃣ VIP Guest Overview

This dashboard identifies the most valuable guests using a combination of frequency, spending, and recency.

#### Guest Segments:

- VIP Guests: Top 5 guests

- Other Guests

#### Metrics Analyzed:

- Share of total booking revenue

- Average booking amount

- Average number of bookings

#### Key Insight:
VIP guests generate disproportionate value through higher spending and repeat bookings, making them critical for long-term revenue stability.
****

---

## 📈 Overall Insights

- High-value guests are limited in number but contribute a large share of total revenue

- Frequent and VIP guests consistently show higher average booking values

- Recent booking activity strongly correlates with increased spending

- Revenue growth opportunities lie more in retention and personalization than in guest volume alone
